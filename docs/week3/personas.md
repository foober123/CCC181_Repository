## Manang In-In, small beverage business owner that offers delivery 

> "Nalimtan nako ang order. ugma nalang nako na i-deliver"

*Photo or sketch* | ![p](images/in-in.webp) |
| *Age, role and background* | 44, Runs a small business that delivers beverages to restaurants and stores                   |
| *Goals*                    | Keep Track of Unresolved Orders, Make sure every delivery call gets an answer                 |
| *Frustrations*             | Orders are sometimes kept on paper, Orders that are texted can be forgotten about for the day |
| *Devices and context*      | Tablet inside office taking calls at the same time                         
| *Tech comfort*        | Medium, Knows how to operate a Point of Sales |
| *Accessibility needs* | Older eyes need visibility                    |

### Scenario

Manang In-In opens her business and gets a delivery on call. After receiving the call, she gets the name and address of the customer and gets the order details written onto a receipt. Afterwards, she writes the ticket containing the name and address and saves order details attached to it. Then she tells an employee to load the drinks into a tricycle and marks the order as "In Transit". When the tricycle comes back with the empty bottles and cash, she marks the order as done

### What this means for my interface

1. Because staff prioritize deliveries by location, the interface needs to display delivery addresses prominently.
2. Because staff receive orders from multiple customers, the interface needs to display customer names on ticket cards.
3. Because some orders are closer to the store. the interface needs the ability to change statuses of orders at any order Manang In-In needs   

## Cartso, small business owner of beverage warehouse that only offers pickup

> "Wala lagi to siya ni anhi diri, basin ugma ra"

*Photo or sketch* | ![pa](images/cartso.png) |
| *Age, role and background* | 50, Runs a small business that only takes pick up orders and walk-in                                                      |
| *Goals*                    | Minimize the time customers spend collecting their purchases.                                                             |
| *Frustrations*             | Customers complain because they are served out of sequence, Customers compete for attention instead of following the line |
| *Devices and context*      | Desktop computer inside office                                                                                            |
| *Tech comfort*             | High, Well-versed in spreadsheet software like MS Excel                                                                   |
| *Accessibility needs*      | Older eyes need visibility                                                                                                |

### Scenario

Mr. Cartso opens his store and 3 customers go to the store at the same time. He gives them numbers and logs their names and orders into the application. He then tells an employee the details of #1's orders and to bring them to the front of the warehouse. He then marks #1's order as complete. #2 and #3 follow
